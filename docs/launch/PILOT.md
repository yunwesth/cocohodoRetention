# Cocohodo MVP — pilot worksheet

Status: Prepared; no real shop pilot has been performed.

## Before inviting customers

- [ ] Name the shop operator and support contact.
- [ ] Confirm eligible drinks, reward size/value, exclusions, and online-order policy.
- [ ] Confirm privacy/terms and photo rights; replace draft copy where needed.
- [ ] Complete real SMS and physical iPhone/Android checks in [LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md](../../LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md).
- [ ] Verify live site/app HTTPS addresses and staff access.
- [ ] Train staff with [STAFF_GUIDE.md](STAFF_GUIDE.md).
- [ ] Print a counter sign only after its destination is final. Suggested copy: “A little thank-you for your coffee moments. Join Cocohodo Rewards.” Generate its QR to the final public `/rewards/` URL, not a preview or localhost address.

## Suggested pilot

Invite 5–10 customers over 3–7 operating days. Ask them to join, show their QR, return on another visit, and describe any confusing steps. Record operational friction without phone numbers, member tokens, or verification codes.

## Release decision

- [ ] Staff can operate enrollment assistance, awards, and redemption without developer help.
- [ ] No unresolved balance, duplicate-transaction, or authorization incidents remain.
- [ ] At least one customer returns to the same saved card on a later visit.
- [ ] A content maintainer and technical operator are identified.
- [ ] Each remaining minor issue has an owner and next action.
