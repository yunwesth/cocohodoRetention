# Cocohodo rewards — counter guide

Use this guide for the pilot after services and the shop's final program rules are approved. A page labeled PREVIEW cannot save real stamps.

## Start a shift

1. Open `app.<approved-domain>/cashier/login` over HTTPS on the shop device.
2. Sign in with the authorized shop account. Customer sign-in does not grant staff access.
3. Check the internet connection and allow camera access when needed. Keep the staff device with staff and sign out at shift end.

## Help a customer join

Direct them to the public website's Rewards page. In the live program, they enter their own phone number and SMS code on their phone, then open My QR. Never ask them to read a verification code aloud or use the shop's staff login. A new digital card starts empty.

If a code does not arrive, confirm the entered number and wait for the resend timer. Ask the shop operator for help if SMS remains unavailable. Do not promise a saved reward when the app has not confirmed it.

## Award stamps

1. Ask the customer to open My QR.
2. Scan it. If scanning fails or camera access is denied, use manual member-code entry.
3. Confirm the number of eligible paid drinks under the shop's approved rules.
4. Submit once and wait for the success message. Confirm the new balance.

Example: a customer with eight stamps buys three eligible drinks. The card reaches ten with one pending stamp. Redeem the full card before adding further purchases. External ordering services do not automatically award stamps.

## Redeem

Resolve the customer's card, confirm it is full, serve the approved reward, and use the redemption action. A free drink does not earn a stamp. Saved overflow moves to the next card after redemption.

## If the connection drops

If the app offers “Confirm saved transaction,” use that recovery action on the same device. It reuses the request ID to avoid duplicate stamps. Do not submit a fresh transaction, clear browser storage, or switch devices to retry an uncertain outcome. Ask the operator for help if it cannot be resolved.

## Escalate to the operator

Wrong balances, account recovery, failed authorization, persistent SMS failures, and suspected duplicate awards require operator assistance. This MVP has no cashier undo/refund or phone-change workflow. Never attempt to repair a balance by awarding compensating stamps without an approved procedure.
